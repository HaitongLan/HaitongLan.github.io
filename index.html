Haitong Lan （蓝海桐）

I am a first-year master’s student in Computer Science and Technology at ShanghaiTech University, advised by Prof. Ziping Zhao. I began my graduate studies in September 2026.

My research interests include optimization for statistical inference, optimization with low-rank and sparse structures, and optimization over probability spaces and distributions.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). From June 2024 to June 2025, I was a visiting student in the group of Prof. Junjie Ma at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science (AMSS).

I welcome conversations about shared research interests.
