Research interests

These directions concern optimization problems in statistical inference, structured models, and spaces of probability distributions.

Optimization for statistical inference

Statistical inference often requires solving optimization problems to estimate unknown parameters or latent structure from data. Key questions include how to design objectives that reflect the statistical model and algorithms that remain reliable under noise and limited observations.

Optimization with Low-Rank and Sparse Structures

Low-rank and sparse structures appear in many estimation and recovery problems. The central question is how to use these structures to improve identifiability, computational efficiency, and robustness.

Optimization on probability spaces and distributions

When the variables are probability distributions, optimization takes place over a space of measures rather than a finite-dimensional parameter set. This raises questions about suitable objectives, constraints, and algorithms for comparing and transforming distributions.
